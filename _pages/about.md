I hold a BSc in Physics from the University of São Paulo (2023) and am currently a PhD candidate at the same institution, under the supervision of Prof. Jorge Ernesto Horvath. My doctoral project is titled "Compact Object Binaries in the Galaxy and the Resulting Mass Distributions".

I have a strong interest in the study of objects in the Lower Mass Gap. To that end, I study the physics of Spider Systems as producers of Black Holes in the Lower Mass Gap through the accretion-induced collapse of a Neutron Star, using the MESA code for this purpose. I am also interested in the observation of Spider Systems across multiple bands of the electromagnetic spectrum.

I am a member of the Multimessenger Astronomy working group of the enhanced X-ray Timing and Polarimetry (eXTP) mission.

My full CV can be found [here](/cv/).

Feel free to reach out! Outside the office, I enjoy reading, hiking, and spending time with friends and family. I'm always happy to talk about science or about life!
